# Severity Rubric

## Critical

- Production-breaking issue
- Security or privacy exposure
- High-likelihood data loss or corruption
- Unsafe device behavior in IoT contexts

## High

- Major user-facing failure
- Strong regression risk in a core flow
- Missing controls around auth, payments, device safety, or critical data logic
- Delivery risk likely to affect a committed milestone

## Medium

- Noticeable defect with workaround
- Moderate reliability, performance, or operational issue
- Missing tests around important but non-critical behavior

## Low

- Minor bug
- Small maintainability gap
- Documentation gap with limited delivery impact
